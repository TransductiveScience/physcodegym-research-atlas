Evidence method / dependencies

What follows from what?

A1–A3Primitive slate

Propagating surface, measurable geometry, finite tension.

DEFINEDTransport calculus

Potential A, curvature K and Hodge operation.

ASSERTEDDynamics

Vacuum and sourced transport equations.

TO BE PROVEDObservable transport

Energy balance, flux scaling and measurable relations.

The first distinction to maintain is between definitions, identities that hold automatically, and physical laws requiring independent premises. The second is between an integrated flux and a pointwise current, which require mathematically compatible differential-form degrees.

For an individual-centered, source-specific examination of the formal claims, see the derivation review. The complete challenge counterexample has not been published here.
