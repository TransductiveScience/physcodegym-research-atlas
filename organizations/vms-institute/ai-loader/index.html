Artificial Intelligence Loader

The institute’s separately disclosed computational packaging and control architecture for embedding rule frameworks in AI systems.

Software methodology / not a physical experiment

A source framework becomes a structured package

The institute proposes decomposing a scientific framework into atomic components: primitive definitions, dependency-linked derivations, measurement and calibration anchors, reproducibility checks and falsifiable predictions. These components are then packed for AI consumption with lexicon mappings and registered scale constraints.

The public description of the Loader emphasizes ingestion, version integrity, terminology stability, runtime validation and recovery. Its claimed use is broader than VMS physics, extending to arbitrary machine-readable governance, compliance or audit rule sets.

01Extract primitives

Terms, assumptions and dependencies.

02Pack and lock

Compressed graph, integrity information and anchors.

03Verify ingestion

Quote-back, enumeration, target calculations.

04Monitor output

Drift detection, errors, recovery paths.

Verification layers

What the specification describes

Among its described methods are cryptographic integrity checks, exact-text quote-back, cross-referenced primitive enumeration, calibration invariant enforcement, holdout calculations and monitoring of drift in output terminology. The patent specification also discusses error propagation, domain boundaries, delivery architectures and an ordering controller.

Those are disclosed design claims. The existence of a published specification does not establish that every described mechanism is deployed, technically effective, novel or patented.

Availability and rights

Public edition, patent application, separate rights

The institute offers a public Loader package under CC BY 4.0 and separately advertises licensing for the patent application family. See the patent and licensing dossier for filing details and status limits.
