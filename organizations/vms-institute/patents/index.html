Patents & licensing

A careful record of the institute’s published filing claims, specifications and the difference between applications, public code and patent rights.

Patent family A / AI package

Artificial Intelligence Loader

VMS Institute identifies U.S. application 19/728,053, filed 1 July 2026, confirmation 5055, docket 61525-0004001. Its public record lists 20 claims, including three independent claims, prepared by Fish & Richardson P.C., and priority claims to provisional applications 63/878,337 and 63/899,158. Its site reproduces a purported USPTO receipt and the application’s specification/drawings, while stating that the claims themselves were not reproduced on the page.

The described invention concerns machine-readable scientific framework ingestion, atomic proposition packaging, compressed semantic relationships, staged validation, calibration locks and model-output drift monitoring. The site says the full U.S. application is expected to publish in March 2027.

Patent family B / physical calibration

Refining Fundamental Asymmetries in Physical Models

The separate U.S. application 19/802,308 is described as filed 9 September 2026 with 22 claims and three independent claims. The institute also lists international application PCT/US26/44857 from that date. This is described as a physical-model calibration/control program, not merely a continuation of the Loader application. Its page lists a projected March 2027 publication date.

The details above are drawn from the institute's posted filing record. An independent current USPTO/PCT registry determination is not established here.

Licensing distinction

Public text is not the same as a patent license

The institute says its AI Loader public edition is available under CC BY 4.0, with DOI 10.5281/zenodo.17699757. It separately offers licenses under its claimed patent family. Reproducing source text under a copyright license and having rights under a granted patent are different questions.

Application ≠ grant. Filing an application does not, by itself, give the applicant a granted patent or establish that another person’s implementation infringes. Patent scope depends on claims as granted, applicable law and facts. The institute’s pages do not establish an existing grant for these 2026 applications.
