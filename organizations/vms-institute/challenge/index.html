The $50,000 VMS Challenge

Published falsification criteria, their provisional status and the two proposed submission routes.

Proposed scientific contest

Two routes to a challenge

The institute advertises a cash award of US$50,000 for a demonstrable mathematical break or prediction failure. In the first category, a challenger identifies an exact step where a derivation does not follow from its stated premises. In the second, the challenger identifies a numbered prediction and compares it with specified measurements, calculations and uncertainty conditions.

The published criteria reject purely authority-based disagreement and requests a precise argument from the same mathematical or empirical premises. This describes the rules the institute proposes; it does not constitute independent endorsement of their adjudication or funding.

ROUTE AMathematical break

Exact equation, premise, conclusion, counterexample or derivation.

ROUTE BPrediction failure

Precise test, data source, uncertainty and mismatch.

Rules and status

Draft pending panel ratification

The challenge page describes the criteria as draft pending ratification by a Technical Evaluation Panel. It proposes independent scientific referees, gives a separate email address for panel applicants, and says submissions should be adjudicated against the published rules. An announced award is not evidence of escrow, a signed payment contract or completed panel selection.
