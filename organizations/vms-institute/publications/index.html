How to read the catalogue

The author's terms “narrative,” “math appendix,” “calibration,” “verification” and “exploratory” describe different document functions. A numerical calibration is not automatically an out-of-sample prediction. The catalogue should therefore be read by tracing definitions, formal deductions, calibration choices and claimed comparisons separately.
