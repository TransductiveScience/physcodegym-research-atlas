Evidence map / limits of calibration

What the framework says is fixed

The VMS appendix expressly separates geometry-only statements from numerical values tied to physical units. In its opening material, the geometric loop action S₀ has units of volume (m³), but its SI interpretation is calibrated by identifying it with ħ at the electron. The paper itself labels this as a calibration lock rather than an independent derivation of ħ or electron mass.

This is an important and useful disclosure. A prediction based on the electron anchor may remain informative, but its uncertainty and independence cannot be assessed as if the electron mass and Planck constant had emerged with no empirical input.

Publication crosswalk

Where the calibration documents live

VMS Institute lists a dedicated Calibration entry and an Electromagnetism Calibration entry. Other appendices and audit blocks point to further calibration narratives. This page records their roles without treating every numerical agreement as a new prediction.

The appendix itself notes that forward numbers produced with unspecified test forms are disclosure-only and cannot be used to falsify the framework. That condition should also prevent counting those same numbers as independent successes before a final predictive form is supplied.
