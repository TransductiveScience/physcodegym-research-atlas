Result / source examination

A precise coverage finding

The institute's Mathematical Bridge: Math Appendix states a chain from its three primitive postulates to transport geometry, vacuum dynamics, wave propagation, energy flux and sources. The published SymPy suite contains 32 modules, many of them testing standard physical identities and equations. This study finds related computations for several stages, but no complete executable derivation of any of the nine VMS-specific claim groups from the primitive void postulates in this particular ZIP.

This does not imply that all 32 modules contain incorrect algebra. Nor does it establish that the source derivations are false. It identifies which additional implications need source-specific verification before a claim of comprehensive automated proof can be made.

Core distinction / proof obligations

Related algebra is not the missing bridge

F0002 assumes the Poisson potential framework and verifies an inverse-square result within it. F0005 uses a conventional gauge-field action and checks tensor/variation identities. F0008 uses the established Rydberg/SI spectroscopy inputs for Balmer comparisons. F0015 and F0025 test Poynting energy conservation, which is a different formal current from the one expressly defined in Section 7 of the VMS Appendix. F0014 algebraically verifies a specified loop-mass functional without deriving that mass functional from a propagating spacetime void.

The calculations can be useful internal checks. Yet each leaves open the distinctive claim that void-boundary geometry necessitates those laws without importing them.
