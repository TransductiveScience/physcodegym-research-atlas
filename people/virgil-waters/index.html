02 / Ideas presented publicly

Existence, voids and closed paths

In the published development narrative, a void is proposed as a propagating absence of spacetime with a dynamical boundary. A closed or curved void trajectory is proposed as the source of mass. A hypothetical earlier massless cosmic event is introduced to explain the geometric irregularities needed to form the first closed structures.

These are the author's propositions. The open technical task is to identify where an existence postulate becomes a precise action principle, where conservation equations are proved, and which independently measurable quantities are generated without calibration to the target result.

04 / Research directions

Questions worth answering from the primary text

  1. What geometric structure exists before a localized void can be assigned an action?
  2. Are the transport quantities assigned their correct differential-form degrees in four spacetime dimensions?
  3. Which additional assumptions, if any, guarantee each stated conservation law?
  4. Does a physical observable remain invariant under the gauge transformations explicitly admitted by the theory?
  5. Which prediction is genuinely independent of the calibration anchors used to specify the model?

Each question can be answered with definitions, equations and computations. None requires an attack on an individual.
