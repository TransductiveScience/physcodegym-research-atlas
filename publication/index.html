08 November 2026 · Publication status

The atlas is online

This site is publicly served at https://physics.transductive.science/ and includes independent articles on simulated energy excess, material interpenetration, invisible contact geometry, counterfactual testing, validation, model comparison, and experimental extensions.

Each article links back to the source research conversation, the authors’ paper, and the TransductiveScience fork.

Persistent service architecture

LayerImplementation
Public hostnamephysics.transductive.science, proxied DNS in Cloudflare.
Inbound routingNamed Cloudflare Tunnel, configured for HTTP/2 transport.
Web originCaddy serving static, source-linked documents from a dedicated site directory.
Restart recoveryTwo enabled systemd user services, automatic restart policies, and user lingering across reboot.
IndexingPublic XML sitemap, robots.txt and JSON research index.
Audit evidencePrivate, local deployment receipt with page hashes, live HTTP checks and verified internal links.

Static publication does not depend on an application database, a model API, or browser sessions. As with any self-hosted tunnel, public availability still depends on a functioning host, Internet connection, DNS and Cloudflare service.

What “permanent” means here

The DNS record, named tunnel credentials, Caddy configuration, article files and boot-enabled service definitions persist after this chat closes. The deployment is designed to restart automatically if the process fails or the host reboots. This is persistent publication infrastructure, not a claim of guaranteed uninterrupted uptime or third-party archival preservation.

Future research can be added as further dated, source-backed articles with a session link and a clear distinction between measurement, interpretation, hypothesis and independent reproduction.
