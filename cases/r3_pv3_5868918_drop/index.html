Questions for an independent test

The following are proposed diagnostic experiments inferred from the case’s recorded motion-family tags. They are not the authors’ scored criteria and have not been run on a candidate reconstruction.

  1. Impact: Audit momentum transfer and energy loss; inspect impulse and collision-pair trajectories.
  2. Perturbation: Offset the impact point and repeat; test whether the resulting contact sequence changes causally.
  3. Free-flight: Compare vertical acceleration and release time with a gravity-only baseline.
  4. Impact response: Perturb drop height and measure bounce timing, energy dissipation and solid contact.

For a reproducible finding: attach the exact candidate program, Blender version, initial conditions, numerical trace, original and altered trial results, and an independently checked control.

Epistemic boundary

Dataset metadata can specify what was observed and how the video is packaged; it cannot by itself identify the unique physical mechanism. It also cannot tell us whether a particular coding agent's output violates physics. Both require an executable scene or calibrated physical experiment.
