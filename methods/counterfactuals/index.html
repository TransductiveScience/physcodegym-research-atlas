Method 01 / Causal interrogation

Change the experiment, expose the mechanism

Counterfactual testing asks what a generated world does outside the original video trajectory.

Why matching a movie is not enough

A particular sequence of images can be produced by many incompatible internal mechanisms: keyframed motion, invisible supports, wrong collision hulls, incorrect forces, or a valid physical model. A single observed trajectory does not uniquely identify the generating equations.

PhysCodeGym’s intervention procedure changes an initial condition, a force, or contact configuration while retaining a measured baseline and a control. This asks the reconstruction to make a prediction beyond the observations that shaped it.

The billiards witness

01Ordinary trajectory

The reconstructed ball rolls plausibly across the table and passes its original-condition checks.

02Intervene

Redirect the cue ball beyond the visible tabletop without supplying new material support.

03Compare

The generated candidate remains suspended; a finite-support control falls under gravity.

The counterfactual reveals an erroneous support boundary that the original trajectory never exercised.

Scope and limits

43 of 156 tasks contain counterfactual validity checks. For those tasks, the published validity workflow assigns 50 points to ordinary criteria and 50 to counterfactual criteria, followed by failure caps. These are task-specific evaluations, not a universal guarantee that a reconstruction obeys physics.

A meaningful follow-up is to choose interventions that maximize disagreement among several competing physical models.
